You don’t need more lessons. You need a plan.

A one-off lesson fixes something for a week, then old habits creep back in. Our tailored Improvement programmes bring your coaching, practice and progress together into one 12-week plan, so every session builds on the last.

Structured programmes to keep you focused

  • Coaching

    Expert coaching built around your game

    6, 12 or 24 one-to-one lessons with a PGA coach, a plan set from your swing evaluation, and Trackman data and video after every session

  • Practice

    Practice with a purpose

    Membership included (5, 8 or 16 practice sessions a month, depending on tier), Trackman bays, and practice set by your coach between lessons

  • Progress

    See your progress, don’t guess it

    Your numbers tracked over the 12 weeks, on-course rounds built into the plan, and clear checkpoints with your coach

Also part of your programme

  • Lessons, practice and membership in one

    Your coaching, practice sessions and membership come together in a single purchase, with no separate bookings to juggle.

  • A plan recommended by your coach

    After your free swing evaluation, your coach talks through your goals and recommends the programme that fits your game.

  • Data and video after every lesson

    Trackman data and video from each lesson are sent to your app, so you can revisit exactly what you worked on.

  • Skills tests and the Trackman Combine

    Benchmark your game with skills tests and the Trackman Combine, and follow your numbers with online performance tracking.

  • Guest passes

    Bring friends along to practise with you: 2 guest passes with Silver and 4 with Gold.

  • Member golf days and Order of Merit

    Join member golf days, climb the Order of Merit and use either of our two City locations.

  • Keep going after week 12

    When your programme ends you roll onto the matching monthly membership, with all the practice tools you need to keep improving.

  • Coming spring 2027

    Workbook and journal

    A programme workbook and journal to plan your sessions and keep track of what’s working.
